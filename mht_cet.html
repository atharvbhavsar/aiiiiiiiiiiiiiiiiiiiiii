About MHT-CET

MHT-CET (Maharashtra Common Entrance Test) is a state-level entrance examination conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra for admission to Engineering and Technology, Pharmacy and Agricultural Education courses offered by various colleges in Maharashtra.

Exam Date: May 2023
Application Deadline: April 2023
Official Website: cetcell.mahacet.org

Exam Pattern

Physics & Chemistry

50 questions each, 100 marks each

Mathematics

50 questions, 100 marks

Total Duration

180 minutes (90 minutes for PCM each)

Marking Scheme

+2 for correct answer, no negative marking

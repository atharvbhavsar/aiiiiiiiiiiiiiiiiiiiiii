Understanding Fee Structure

🏛️ Government Colleges

  • • Tuition Fee: ₹10,000 - ₹50,000/year
  • • Hostel Fee: ₹20,000 - ₹40,000/year
  • • Mess Fee: ₹15,000 - ₹25,000/year
  • • Other Charges: ₹5,000 - ₹15,000/year
  • • Total: ₹50,000 - ₹1,30,000/year

🏢 Private Colleges

  • • Tuition Fee: ₹1,00,000 - ₹3,00,000/year
  • • Hostel Fee: ₹40,000 - ₹80,000/year
  • • Mess Fee: ₹25,000 - ₹40,000/year
  • • Other Charges: ₹20,000 - ₹50,000/year
  • • Total: ₹1,85,000 - ₹4,70,000/year

💡 Financial Planning Tips

Before Admission

  • • Research total cost including hostel and mess
  • • Check if you qualify for any scholarships
  • • Compare fees across multiple colleges
  • • Consider distance from home (travel costs)
  • • Plan for additional expenses (books, projects)

During Studies

  • • Apply for multiple scholarships
  • • Look for part-time opportunities
  • • Consider education loans if needed
  • • Track your expenses monthly
  • • Save money for emergencies

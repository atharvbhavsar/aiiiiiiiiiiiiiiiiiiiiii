Option Form Guide

📋 What is an Option Form?

An option form is where you list your preferred colleges and branches in order of preference. This determines your admission chances.

🎯 How to Prioritize Colleges

  • • Rank by preference: Put your dream college first
  • • Consider cutoffs: Check if your percentile meets the requirements
  • • Location preference: Consider distance from home
  • • Fee structure: Ensure it fits your budget
  • • Placement records: Check average packages
  • • Branch preference: Choose your preferred engineering branch

⚠️ Important Tips

  • • Fill maximum options: Don't leave slots empty
  • • Realistic choices: Include colleges where you have good chances
  • • Backup options: Include some safer choices
  • • Branch variety: Consider multiple branches
  • • Deadline: Submit before the last date
